CLEAN CULTURE

About us

Clean Culture is a modern daily essentials delivery platform designed to simplify everyday household purchases. Customers can order fresh milk, tender coconuts, protein meals, RO water, oils, and other essentials.

The platform supports both one-time orders and flexible product subscriptions. It connects customers, vendors, products, payments, orders, and deliveries in one seamless ecosystem. Our goal was to create a convenient, reliable, and predictable daily delivery experience.

Challenges

Engineering the Clean Culture platform required building a high-concurrency delivery engine capable of handling complex session management, real-time inventory validation, and dynamic checkout calculations.

High-Concurrency Traffic & Session Management
Real-Time Pincode & Serviceability Validation
Automated Subscription & Daily Delivery Engine
Multi-Cart & Zero-Latency Reordering Architecture
Dynamic GST, Coupon & Delivery Calculations
Unified Payment Gateway & Order Tracking Pipeline

Solution

To address high-concurrency delivery challenges, our team architected isolated checkout sessions, a centralized calculation engine, and strict stock validation with realistic visual interfaces.

Scope

Explore the core operational scope of the Clean Culture platform — engineered for performance, reliability, and continuous scalability.

01 Storefront & Catalog

Product Discovery Pages

Rich product listings featuring multi-weight selectors (100g, 250g, 500g packs), real-time stock availability badges, and fluid search filters engineered for instant conversion.

02 Checkout Core

Dynamic Cart Engine

High-performance cart management supporting multi-item weight calculations, real-time total updates, discount coupon application, and instant weight package bundling.

03 Security & Profiles

User Authentication

Frictionless onboarding experience powered by secure OTP phone verification, Google One-Tap Sign-In, saved session persistence, and unified account profile settings.

04 Logistics & COD

Address Book & Serviceability

Instant pincode validation engine that checks delivery serviceability in real-time, calculates estimated delivery days, and dynamically enables or disables Cash-on-Delivery (COD).

05 Order Lifecycle

Order Tracking & History

Comprehensive order management dashboard providing real-time status updates, past invoice downloads, itemized receipts, and a 1-click repeat reorder pipeline.

06 Gateway Integration

Secure Multi-Step Checkout

Bank-grade checkout pipeline integrated with Razorpay payment gateway, supporting UPI, credit/debit cards, net banking, automated stock hold, and instant order confirmation.
